## Inspiration

Official EvoHacks 2 theme: **Projects for Social Good: build something that makes a real difference for people and communities.**

International students waste nights on opportunities that quietly exclude them. VisaFit pastes official rules and returns eligible / skip / uncertain with hard reason codes for a UK student-visa, final-year, London profile, so builders spend the hack on the theme instead of dead-end eligibility.

## How it works

1. Paste rules text
2. Confirm profile flags
3. Get a verdict + reason codes
